# Cartland Labs Privacy Policy

_Effective Date: August 17th, 2026_

**Important Note:** Cartland Labs is an experimental app made available to a small number of users. It is used to explore and experiment with new application features, and its functionality may change at any time.

## Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

- Activity data: How you use the app and its features, so we can make the service better.
- Submitted content: Any data you submit through the app, including text, images, videos, and other files.

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Improve the service: Analyze activity to identify bugs, optimize features, and enhance user experience.
- Develop new features: Experiment with new application features and evaluate how well they work.

## Data Sharing

We do not share your information with any third parties.

## Your Choices

You have the following choices regarding your data:

- Delete your app: This will remove all stored data from your device.
- Request deletion: Contact us at [chris@cart.land](mailto:chris@cart.land) to request deletion of data you have submitted.

## Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Data is stored using established cloud infrastructure with proper security practices in place. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

## Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children.

## Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions, please contact us at [chris@cart.land](mailto:chris@cart.land).
